In the apartment

Air conditioning

Available in the main areas. To keep it working well, close doors and windows while it is on, and turn it off when you leave the apartment.

High-speed wifi

A stable connection for remote work and streaming. The network name and password are provided at registration. This usually does not happen, but if the connection drops, restart wifi on your device; if it still fails, contact us right away on WhatsApp.

2 Smart TVs

One in the living room and one in the bedroom. They have cable TV with national and international channels, plus internet access so you can sign in with your own streaming platforms. Remember to log out before checkout.

Workspace

A desk and area set up for comfortable, focused work.

Equipped kitchen

Refrigerator, stove, microwave oven, dishware, coffee maker, glasses, and basic kitchen utensils.

Hot water

In the shower and sinks in both bathrooms. You will receive instructions to turn on the hot water before adjusting any technical controls.

Washer

In the in-unit laundry area. Use a moderate amount of detergent, do not overload the drum, and remove clothes as soon as the cycle finishes. Check the operating instructions.

Essentials

Full sets of clean bed sheets, towels for registered guests, bath soap, shampoo, and toilet paper. If anyone needs blankets, they are available.

Iron and hangers

An iron for clothes, and you’ll find hangers in the closet.
